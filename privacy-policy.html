1. Introduction

ViviTap is a communication assistance application designed for people with communication disabilities and their caregivers ("helpers"). It serves users of all ages, including minors who use the app under parental or guardian supervision. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO) and applicable German law.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • Email address (Firebase Authentication)
  • Username (chosen at registration)
  • Display name (optional)
  • Profile photo (optional, stored in Firebase Storage)
  • User role: helpee or helper

2.2 Communication Data

  • Messages sent and received between connected users (last 50 messages per contact, stored in Firestore)
  • Custom communication buttons (text, emoji, optional image)
  • Voice recordings attached to communication buttons, recorded in the app to replace synthesised speech (audio files, stored in Firebase Storage)
  • Voice messages recorded by a helper and played on the helpee's device (audio files, stored in Firebase Storage)
  • Delivery status of messages: whether a message has reached the recipient's device, and whether it has been heard (by a helpee) or seen on screen (by a helper), with the time of each step
  • Button usage statistics (frequency of use per button)
  • Voice and video calls between connected users. The call itself, sound and image, is transmitted live and is never recorded. A short call record (caller, recipient, time, status, duration and whether it was a video call) is kept for at most 24 hours, together with a counter of the call minutes used each month. During a call, a helpee can send buttons from their grid: the button's text, emoji and voice clip are passed live to the other person and are not stored.

2.3 Technical Data

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push token for notifications
  • Microphone access, requested only when you first start a recording or a call. ViviTap never records in the background: recording begins when you tap the record button and stops when you release it or when the time limit is reached. During a call, the microphone is used only while the call is in progress and stops as soon as it ends.
  • Camera access, requested only the first time a camera is turned on during a video call. The camera is used only while it is turned on during a call: it never films outside a call, it switches off when the app goes to the background, and the image is never recorded. A helpee's camera can only be used if video has been explicitly allowed in the call settings of their account; otherwise their device sends sound only.
  • Last activity timestamp, used to show your connected contacts whether you are currently using the app
  • App preferences: language, grid layout, dark mode, audio volume, send method
  • Connection list (usernames of connected caregivers/users)
  • Crash reports and anonymized diagnostic data (Firebase Crashlytics)

2.4 Subscription Data

  • Subscription status and plan (free, Family, Pro)
  • Purchase history managed by RevenueCat
  • An anonymized user identifier shared with RevenueCat for subscription management

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core app functionality: sending messages, communication buttons, managing contacts
  • To deliver push notifications for incoming messages and calls. An incoming call notification shows the caller's name, including on the lock screen, so that the call can be answered
  • To connect voice and video calls between connected users, and to count call minutes against the limits of each plan (a minute of video counts as two)
  • To store and play back voice recordings: button recordings on the device that plays them, voice messages on the recipient’s device
  • To show the sender whether a message has been received, heard or seen, and to show connected contacts whether you are currently using the app
  • To restore your preferences across sessions
  • To manage subscriptions and enforce plan limits
  • To monitor app stability and fix crashes
  • To enable account deletion and data erasure upon request

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6 & 9)

  • Art. 6(1)(b) — Performance of a contract: core app functionality
  • Art. 6(1)(a) — Consent: optional data such as profile photo
  • Art. 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interest: security, fraud prevention, app stability

Regarding health-related data: the use of a communication assistance application may implicitly reveal a communication disability. This constitutes special category data under GDPR Art. 9. We process this data solely to provide the app's core services, based on explicit consent given at registration (Art. 9(2)(a)). This explicit consent is collected through a dedicated consent screen at registration, where users must actively check three separate boxes: (1) acceptance of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, (2) acknowledgement that ViviTap may process data relating to their health or disability situation, and (3) confirmation of age eligibility. The consent record, including timestamp and version of the documents presented, is stored securely and associated with the user's account.

Voice recordings warrant particular mention. A recording of someone's voice may reveal a speech or communication impairment far more directly than written text does. Such recordings fall under the same explicit consent and are used for one purpose only: playing the message back to the recipient it was addressed to. We do not analyse them, transcribe them, or use them to identify anyone. Recording is always initiated by the user, never automatically.

Voice and video calls fall under the same explicit consent. Like a voice, a person's image may reveal a disability. A call carries the voice, and in a video call the image, live from one person to the other; it is never recorded, transcribed or analysed, by ViviTap or by anyone else. A call only starts when someone places it, and only connects when the other person answers. A camera only films while it is turned on, and a helpee's camera only once video has been explicitly allowed for their account.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Firebase (Google LLC)

We use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Firebase Crashlytics. User data (Firestore, Storage) is stored on servers located in europe-west3 (Frankfurt, Germany). Cloud Functions are deployed in europe-west1 (Belgium) and process data transiently without storing it. Crash reports may contain device type, OS version, and anonymized stack traces. More information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5.2 Google Sign-In (Google LLC)

If you choose to sign in with Google, your Google account email address and a Google-issued identifier are used solely for authentication purposes. More information: policies.google.com/privacy

5.3 RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.)

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat processes your subscription status, purchase history, and an anonymized user identifier. Payments are handled by Google Play. RevenueCat applies SCCs for EU data transfers. More information: revenuecat.com/privacy

5.4 LiveKit (LiveKit Inc.)

We use LiveKit to carry voice and video calls between connected users. Our LiveKit project is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). LiveKit relays the call audio and video in real time and does not store them: calls are never recorded. Each participant is identified to LiveKit by an opaque technical identifier, never by name or email address; the name of the call room, however, contains the usernames of both participants. LiveKit keeps technical session data (when a call took place, the room name and the participants' identifiers) under its Data Processing Addendum. To keep calls fast, the audio and video may be routed through the nearest LiveKit server, which can be outside the EU; they are encrypted in transit and never stored there.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained as long as the account is active
  • Messages: last 50 messages per contact; deleted when either user deletes their account
  • Voice messages: deleted together with the message they belong to, under the same limit of 50 messages per contact. Files left behind by an interrupted upload, which no message refers to, are removed automatically within one year at most.
  • Button voice recordings: retained as long as the button exists; deleted when you delete the button or your account
  • Push tokens: deleted upon account deletion
  • Calls: the sound and image of calls are never stored. Call records are deleted automatically within 24 hours. The monthly counter of call minutes is kept as long as the account is active. Anonymous monthly statistics (number and length of calls, without any identifier) may be kept indefinitely.
  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days
  • Consent records: retained for the duration of the account plus 3 years after deletion (GDPR Art. 7)
  • Subscription history: retained by RevenueCat per their own retention policy and legal obligations
  • All data held by ViviTap: permanently deleted upon account deletion (Settings → Security → Delete Account)

You have the right to: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17) via Settings → Security → Delete Account, restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), object to processing (Art. 21), and to withdraw consent at any time.

Contact us at contact@munyroth.com. We will respond within one month (Art. 12 GDPR).

8. International Data Transfers

Two third-party services may transfer data outside the EU: RevenueCat, and LiveKit, whose call audio and video may be routed through a server outside the EU without being stored there, and some of whose technical sub-processors are based in the United States. Such transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

9. Minors

ViviTap is not a children's app, but it may be used by individuals under 16 years of age — including people with communication disabilities — under the supervision and with the explicit consent of a parent or legal guardian. In such cases, the parent or guardian creates and manages the account and is responsible for ensuring appropriate use.

By creating an account, you confirm that you are at least 16 years old, or that a parent or guardian has reviewed and accepted these terms and provided explicit consent on behalf of the minor.

10. Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS. Firebase infrastructure provides encryption at rest. Access to user data is restricted to authenticated users via Firebase security rules and Cloud Functions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes within the app. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

Concernant les données de santé : l'utilisation d'une application d'aide à la communication peut implicitement révéler un handicap de communication. Ces données relèvent de l'Art. 9 RGPD (données de catégorie spéciale). Nous les traitons uniquement pour fournir les services de base, sur la base d'un consentement explicite donné à l'inscription (Art. 9(2)(a)). Ce consentement est recueilli via un écran dédié avec trois cases à cocher : (1) acceptation des CGU et de la présente politique, (2) reconnaissance du traitement des données liées au handicap, (3) confirmation de l'âge. L'enregistrement du consentement (horodatage, version des documents) est conservé de manière sécurisée.

Les enregistrements vocaux méritent une mention particulière. L'enregistrement de la voix d'une personne peut révéler un trouble de la parole ou de la communication bien plus directement qu'un texte écrit. Ces enregistrements relèvent du même consentement explicite et servent à une seule fin : jouer le message au destinataire auquel il était adressé. Nous ne les analysons pas, ne les transcrivons pas et ne les utilisons pas pour identifier qui que ce soit. Un enregistrement est toujours déclenché par l'utilisateur, jamais automatiquement.

Les appels audio et vidéo relèvent du même consentement explicite. Comme la voix, l'image d'une personne peut révéler un handicap. Un appel transmet la voix, et pour un appel vidéo l'image, en direct d'une personne à l'autre ; il n'est jamais enregistré, transcrit ni analysé, ni par ViviTap ni par qui que ce soit. Un appel ne démarre que si quelqu'un le lance, et ne s'établit que si l'autre personne répond. Une caméra ne filme que lorsqu'elle est allumée, et celle d'une personne aidée seulement une fois la vidéo expressément autorisée pour son compte.

Sprachaufnahmen verdienen eine besondere Erwähnung. Die Aufnahme der Stimme einer Person kann eine Sprech- oder Kommunikationsbeeinträchtigung weit unmittelbarer offenbaren als geschriebener Text. Solche Aufnahmen unterliegen derselben ausdrücklichen Einwilligung und dienen einem einzigen Zweck: der Wiedergabe der Nachricht für den Empfänger, an den sie gerichtet war. Wir analysieren sie nicht, transkribieren sie nicht und verwenden sie nicht zur Identifizierung von Personen. Eine Aufnahme wird stets von der Nutzerin oder dem Nutzer ausgelöst, niemals automatisch.

Sprach- und Videoanrufe unterliegen derselben ausdrücklichen Einwilligung. Wie die Stimme kann auch das Bild einer Person eine Behinderung erkennen lassen. Ein Anruf überträgt die Stimme, bei einem Videoanruf auch das Bild, live von einer Person zur anderen und wird niemals aufgezeichnet, transkribiert oder analysiert – weder von ViviTap noch von Dritten. Ein Anruf beginnt nur, wenn jemand ihn startet, und kommt nur zustande, wenn die andere Person annimmt. Eine Kamera filmt nur, solange sie eingeschaltet ist, und die einer betreuten Person erst, wenn Video für ihr Konto ausdrücklich erlaubt wurde.

Wir nutzen LiveKit, um Sprach- und Videoanrufe zwischen verbundenen Nutzern zu übertragen. Unser LiveKit-Projekt wird in der Europäischen Union (Frankfurt, Deutschland) betrieben. LiveKit leitet Ton und Bild des Anrufs in Echtzeit weiter und speichert sie nicht: Anrufe werden niemals aufgezeichnet. Jede teilnehmende Person ist gegenüber LiveKit durch eine nicht sprechende technische Kennung identifiziert, niemals durch Namen oder E-Mail-Adresse; der Name des Anrufraums enthält jedoch die Benutzernamen beider Teilnehmenden. LiveKit speichert technische Sitzungsdaten (wann ein Anruf stattfand, den Raumnamen und die Kennungen der Teilnehmenden) im Rahmen seines Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrags. Damit Anrufe flüssig bleiben, können Ton und Bild über den nächstgelegenen LiveKit-Server geleitet werden, der sich außerhalb der EU befinden kann; sie sind während der Übertragung verschlüsselt und werden dort niemals gespeichert.
